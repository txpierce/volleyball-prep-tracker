Youth Volleyball Nutrition Guide

Fueling for Growth & Explosive Power

At 5'0" and 92 lbs, consistent quality fueling supports natural growth spurts and builds lateral speed for back-row defense. Focus on wholesome meals rather than strict calorie restriction.

Main Meal Components
  • Protein: Chicken, turkey, fish, eggs, Greek yogurt, or lean beef.
  • Complex Carbs: Rice, sweet potatoes, oats, whole wheat bread.
  • Healthy Fats: Avocado, nuts, seeds, olive oil.
Hydration & Electrolytes
  • Drink water steadily throughout the day.
  • Aim for at least 16 oz before court practice.
  • Rehydrate post-practice with water + electrolyte minerals.
