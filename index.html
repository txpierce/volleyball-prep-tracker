YOUTH VOLLEYBALL NUTRITION PROTOCOL

Fueling for Growth & Explosive Power

At 5'0" and 92 lbs, consistent quality fueling supports natural growth spurts and builds lateral speed for back-row defense. Focus on wholesome meal timing rather than strict calorie restriction.

Core Nutrition Pillars
Energy & Carbs ~55%
220–250g Daily
Primary fuel source for quick-burst reactions, court transitions, and alertness.
Protein ~20%
65–85g Daily
Muscle repair, structural bone building, and recovery after jumping & diving.
Healthy Fats ~25%
45–55g Daily
Hormone balance, joint protection, and sustained energy on long tournament days.
Hydration 64–80 oz
Water + Minerals
Sustains muscle contraction velocity and prevents late-match court fatigue.
